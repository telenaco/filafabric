## Added to the plan after Luis's feedback (28 October)

- Time, filament and layers on every card and preset at 100 × 100 mm, from numbers worked out ahead of time (`card-estimates.js`, 12 KB).
- The side-view strip and the "How a drawing prints" guide removed; the page fits the window; Export G-code; cards listed simplest first; a name filter.
- Truly flat versions of the cards that printed two layers: Honeycomb, Re-entrant mesh, Looped rows, Figure-8 rows, Star lattice, Harmonograph. Their two-layer cards moved to Layered (Raised for the two rows cards).
- Single-layer Flat cards without a backing: Truchet tiles, Turing stripes, Meander, Parquet, Bouclé & picots. They carry a "Needs a backing" label.
- Flat versions of the Squiggle strand types (Wave net, Square-wave net, Figure-8 net, Zigzag mesh): the same two families in one layer, joined where they cross. Rings · flat is now one plane as well. Luis decided to keep every build as its own card, on its own shelf, rather than nesting them behind a switch.
- A design list that can be dragged wider to show three or four cards across (remembered in the browser; double-click resets it).
- **Print order on the Flat shelf follows the layered card's.** Luis printed the Honeycomb both ways: the layered one does every row, then the verticals, while the flat one hopped between a row, a few struts and the next row. Honeycomb · flat and Re-entrant mesh · flat now print every row first (one stroke each, alternating direction) and then the struts a column at a time, up one column and down the next. The flat Zigzag mesh, Star lattice and Harmonograph print their layers one after the other too.
- **One line wherever the design allows it.** Lines that meet are joined into the fewest strokes with no line printed twice (Euler trails, `network.js`). Rings · flat prints a row of rings as one line (223 strokes became 36), Spiral cells 320 → 72, Kirigami 3,190 → 143, Isohedral tiles 79 → 29, Arrowhead band 190 → 62, and Stochastic net, Voronoi cells and Meander with ties join what they can. Honeycomb and Re-entrant mesh are already at the fewest strokes without retracing (about half the junctions are odd, so 225 at 100 × 100 mm); going lower would mean printing over lines. Truchet, Turing, Meander, Parquet and Bouclé flat are isolated pieces whose lines never meet, so a single line gains nothing there.
- **"What is counted" lives inside the studio.** `study.html`, the page Google had cached, is gone: its text is a dialog in the studio, opened from the two links that used to go to it, so it sits behind the password. Google's cached copy stays until it is recrawled; Search Console's Removals tool asks for it to be dropped sooner.
- 2D print-order playback with the G-code built in the background, SVG export of the 2D view, and slider minimums that follow the nozzle.

## Known limits, stated plainly

- Grid · sliding with a mixed slide mask can leave nozzle-clearance warnings; the default mask prints in a safe order.
- Wave net · bridged is not fully connected with a 0.2 mm nozzle (recorded in `nozzle-limits.js`).
- Harmonograph · flat joins every place the curve meets itself in the plane; this is a design choice Luis has not confirmed.
- Grids with every crossing lifted, Grid · sliding's steep channel legs and Colour exposure's loose regions are accepted by design and listed in `by-design.js`.
- Not reproduced: the page scrolling beyond the bottom. The page height now follows the window and lists no longer drag the page; if it still happens, the device and window size are needed.
- The nozzle time saved by a single line is small on this profile: travel is under a tenth of the estimated time and the Foamy profile has no retraction or hop. What changes is the number of starts and stops (each one a place for ooze with a flexible filament) and that the print follows the order Luis expects. Rings · flat went from 7.1 to 6.6 min and Spiral cells from 4.4 to 3.8; most cards move by a few seconds.
- Software geometry is not print evidence. No stiffness, drape, recovery or sliding claim has been confirmed.
